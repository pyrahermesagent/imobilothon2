## 1. Introduction and Scope
* **System Name:** Notes App.
* **Purpose:** user-owned note storage.
* **Scope:** the note-ownership domain; auth is a supporting sub-domain.

## 2. Ubiquitous Language (Glossary)
* **User:** an authenticated principal identified by a unique email (`models.py:10`).
* **Note:** a titled text body owned by exactly one User (`models.py:23-29`).
* **Ownership:** the 1:N relation `users` -> `notes` (`models.py:14`, `models.py:26`).

## 3. Bounded Contexts
* **Identity & Access:** email + password authentication (`routes/auth.py:9-15`).
* **Note Storage:** create/read of notes within a user's ownership scope
  (`routes/notes.py:9-21`).

* **Note** — identity: integer id (`models.py:25`); attributes: user_id, title, body,

### 4.3. Aggregates and Aggregate Roots

## 5. Domain Relationships
* **One-to-Many (1:N):** `User` has many `Note` (`models.py:14`, `models.py:26`).

## 6. Business Rules & Invariants

* Email is unique across users (`models.py:10`).
* Passwords are never stored in cleartext (`models.py:11`, `models.py:16-20`).
